The card

SurvivesIncompleteNot digitised
Today
Fez, Qarawiyyin Library, Mushaf no. 902
Format
12 parchment folios; 25 × 28 cm, horizontal format; 11 lines
Script
“Group D” in François Déroche’s classification of the early Abbasid scripts
Origin
al-Andalus or the Maghrib, according to the catalogue of the exhibition Masahif al-Maghrib (2011)

One of the horizontal-format Qur’an fragments in early Abbasid script. The Qarawiyyin Library holds the most important group of them in Morocco, including near-complete copies that have not yet been studied. Umberto Bongianino published an image of one of its leaves (fol. 6r).

Undated; its copyist and place of copying are not known for certain, and its dating to the fourth century AH is an estimate, so it may be later than the Idrisids.
