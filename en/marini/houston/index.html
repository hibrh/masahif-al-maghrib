The Qur’an of Abu Talib al-Juhani

Le Coran d’Abu Talib al-Juhani (Houston) · مصحف أبي طالب الجهني

End of Rabiʿ I 718 AH · c. 1 June 1318 CE

In the reign of Abu Saʿid

Museum record ↗The card on the Marinid Qur’ans page
SurvivesCompleteness unknownPartial images

Its colophon says it belonged to Abu Talib, son of the shaykh Abu Faris ʿAbd al-ʿAziz b. Saʿid b. Ismaʿil b. ʿAbd al-ʿAziz b. Saʿid al-Juhani

Today

Format
Parchment; 28.5 × 28.9 × 6 cm

A dated Maghribi Qur’an from the reign of Sultan Abu Saʿid ʿUthman. It was not written for a sultan but for a notable whom the colophon names with his lineage. It is in Maghribi script on parchment, and its gilded decoration brings out the divisions of the text.

The museum has not published a detailed description saying whether the Qur’an is complete or one volume of a set, or how many lines it has. Images are on the museum’s website; it is not fully digitised.

Museum record

No samples here. The manuscript is not digitised, and we found no published image of it. The drawing shows the layout of the page: number of lines not published.
