Masahif al-Maghrib

Dated Qur’an manuscripts of Morocco with a known copyist or patron: the ones that survive, the ones that are lost, and the ones that can be browsed online today. Each era has a page of its own.

Wattasid Qur’ans

876–961 AH / 1472–1554 CE

A period from which few dated Qur’ans have been studied. It was then that al-Habti set out the pausal system that Maghribi Qur’ans have followed; from it come the Green Qur’an and a Qur’an endowed in Fez in 949 AH.

Criteria

What goes into the guide?

Criteria for the first list of each era

  • The date of copying is stated explicitly in the colophon or the endowment deed.
  • The owner is known: the copyist, or the person for whom or for whose library the Qur’an was made.
  • It was demonstrably copied in the lands of that dynasty.

Each page has two further sections: Qur’ans recorded in the sources and since lost, and well-known Qur’ans on the edge of the criteria, with what each of them lacks.

The samples

The samples are close-ups of the writing itself, taken from Qur’ans digitised by their libraries, from images published under an open licence, or, for Qur’ans with no digitised copy, from studies and books that published them, with the source named under each image. Weak images were enhanced with AI and are marked as such.
