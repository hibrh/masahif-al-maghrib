The twenty-three Qur’ans Mawlay ʿAbd Allah sent to the Prophet’s Mosque

Les vingt-trois Corans offerts par Mawlay ʿAbd Allah à la Mosquée du Prophète · المصاحف الثلاثة والعشرون التي أهداها المولى عبد الله إلى الحرم النبوي

1155 AH · 1742–1743 CE

The card on the Alaouite Qur’ans page
Lost

Al-Zayyani records among the events of 1155 AH that Sultan Mawlay ʿAbd Allah b. Ismaʿil sent with the pilgrim caravan twenty-three Qur’ans, “large and small, all adorned with gold and set with pearls and rubies”, among them “the great ʿUqbani Qur’an” attributed to ʿUqba b. Nafiʿ, which the kings had handed down. We have found none of them whose whereabouts is known today.

Source: Al-Zayyani, al-Bustan al-zarif, cited by al-Manuni (1968)
