Al-Mushaf al-Hasani in the hand of al-Bahawi al-Susi

Le Coran hassanien, de la main d’al-Bahawi al-Soussi · المصحف الحسني بخط البهاوي السوسي

1387 AH (first edition) · 1967 CE

In the reign of al-Hasan II

Printing
Ministry of Habous and Islamic Affairs: first edition 1387 AH / 1967 CE, second 1395 AH / 1975 CE

A Qur’an in mabsūṭ script whose printing King Hassan II ordered, and the one that made the name «المصحف الحسني», al-Mushaf al-Hasani, well known. When al-Mushaf al-Hasani was reprinted in 1417 AH, the royal order stated that the new edition «تمتاز على الطبعات السابقة بتسبيعها», “excels the earlier editions by its division into sevenths”, so the Musabbaʿ Qur’an below is a new edition of al-Mushaf al-Hasani in other hands.

Printed, not manuscript. The dates of its two editions come from the description of the exhibition “Silat al-Maghariba bi-l-janab al-nabawi al-sharif” of the Rabita Muhammadiyya of ʿUlama; we have not seen a copy.

Interview with the researcher Ahmed Saidi (2013, Arabic)
