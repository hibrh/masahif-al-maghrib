About the author

Ibrahim Hassani Idrissi

Teacher of Maghribi scripts and researcher in manuscripts

Ibrahim Hassani Idrissi is a teacher of Maghribi scripts and a researcher in manuscripts, based in Fez. He holds the Certificate of Training in the Art of Calligraphy from the Qarawiyyin (2019), and received the encouragement award of the Mohammed VI Prize for the Art of Maghribi Calligraphy at its nineteenth edition (2025). He has taken part in several national and international exhibitions and competitions.

His interest in Moroccan Qur’ans and manuscripts, and in the history of their scripts, gave rise to this site: a guide to the dated Qur’ans of Morocco’s eras, with close-ups of their writing.

Certificate and award

  • Certificate of Training in the Art of Calligraphy, the Qarawiyyin (2019)
  • Encouragement award of the Mohammed VI Prize for the Art of Maghribi Calligraphy, 19th edition (2025)
  • Several national and international exhibitions and competitions
