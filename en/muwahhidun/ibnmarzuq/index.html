The Qur’an of Ibn Marzuq the Grandfather

Le Coran d’Ibn Marzuq l’Aïeul · مصحف ابن مرزوق الجد

559 AH · 1163–1164 CE

In Valencia under Ibn Mardanish

The card on the Almohad Qur’ans page
SurvivesCompleteness unknownNot digitised
Copyist
Muhammad b. Musa b. Hizb Allah
Place
Valencia
Today
Tetouan, library of the Higher Institute, no. 1. Mohammed al-Manuni saw it in 1959 and Mohammed al-Sharifi in 1973; Bongianino could not find it, and it was most likely moved to the Faculty of Usul al-Din in Tetouan.
Format
Gazelle parchment; medium square format; Andalusi script

Al-Maqqari saw it in Tlemcen in the hands of the descendants of Ibn Marzuq the Grandfather (d. 781 AH), with his handwriting on it; it later reached Morocco.

Its copyist and date are known, but it was copied in Valencia under Ibn Mardanish, before the city came under Almohad rule.

Bongianino 2020, appendix
