The Pink Qur’an

Le Coran rose · المصحف الوردي

Before Safar 650 AH (date of its endowment) · before April–May 1252 CE

The leaf at the Metropolitan Museum ↗The card on the Almohad Qur’ans page
SurvivesIncompletePartial images
Endowment
Endowed by the Caliph al-Murtada in Safar 650 AH to the tomb of one of his wives, “the mother of our son Ishaq”, after his defeat by the Marinids
Place
Probably Marrakesh
Today
Marrakesh, Ibn Yusuf Library ms. 431 (most of the volumes); scattered leaves in the Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York (2017.232), the J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles (Ms. 122), the Museum of Islamic Art, Doha, and elsewhere
Format
Paper dyed pink; ten volumes; five lines to the page

A Qur’an in a monumental script on pink paper, made by two calligraphers and two illuminators in Marrakesh in the last decades of the Almohad state. At the top of its leaves, pinpricks spell the word «حبس», “endowed”, made when the Marinids re-endowed it.

Undated, and no copyist is named. Its latest possible date comes from its endowment deed.
