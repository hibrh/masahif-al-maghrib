### Alt text, image by image

1. Three pages from Moroccan Qur'ans: the colophon of al-Mansur's Qur'an in gold on lapis blue, a page of Abu al-Hasan's rabʿa and a page of the Green Qur'an in silver. Title: Five treasures of Moroccan Qur'an manuscripts.
2. A page from volume 1 of the Caliph al-Murtada's rabʿa (al-Baqara 19–20), in bold Maghribi mabsūṭ in black ink on parchment, with vowels in red.
3. Surat al-Fatiha in the Qur'an of Sultan Abu Yaʿqub Yusuf, in Maghribi mabsūṭ on parchment, with gilded ornaments between the verses.
4. A page of Sultan Abu al-Hasan's rabʿa (al-Saffat 144–147), in a broad, bold mabsūṭ in dark brown ink, vocalised in red, blue and yellow.
5. A page of the Green Qur'an: writing in silver on paper dyed dark green.
6. The colophon of Sultan Ahmad al-Mansur's Qur'an: writing in gold on a lapis blue ground, in an ornamented frame.
7. The name and address of the guide, Masahif al-Maghrib, with its figures: 12 centuries, 7 dynasties and more than 60 Qur'ans, in the colours of the seven dynasties.

## About the images

The manuscript images come from the libraries and museums credited on the site, and each carousel image names its source at the bottom.
The images of Abu al-Hasan's rabʿa, al-Murtada's rabʿa and the Green Qur'an are reproduced at low resolution after Dr Mohamed Abdelhafid Khabta, as on the site, and the two images of al-Mansur's colophon are from Patrimonio Nacional (Spain).
